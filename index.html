01 — Research

What I work on

Proteins do their work by moving. I study how structure and conformational dynamics decide what a protein binds and how it passes a signal on, and how machine learning can predict both.

Research interests

  • Protein–ligand interaction modeling
  • Protein structure representations and embeddings
  • Generative models (diffusion and flow-based methods)
  • Conformational dynamics and receptor activation
