**Lead Developer** &middot; Mar 2026 - present &middot; [Project website](https://cbpl-kit.vercel.app/)

CBPL-kit is an interactive platform for visualizing multistage liquid-liquid extraction
using the Hunter-Nash method.

I led the design and development of the platform, building interactive ternary-diagram
visualizations and stage-by-stage calculations so that students can connect
phase-equilibrium theory with extraction process design.

The tool is scheduled for integration into an undergraduate chemical engineering course
at Seoul National University in Fall 2026.
